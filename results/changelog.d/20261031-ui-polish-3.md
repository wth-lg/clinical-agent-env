- UI polish PR 3 (builder-226): the trial detail. On a phone, the rubric and the Attribution exposure tables show one card per row, on the same table: the item, its points and a met mark on the first line, then the judge quote under a rule. Column heads stay for screen readers, and an empty quote is hidden. From sm up, Points and Met no longer wrap. Met, not met, not triggered and judge unsure each have a mark (check, cross, dash, question mark), with the cross in the danger colour when the row costs points. Each rubric row is named by the question its item asks, as the Tasks view shows it, with the id in small mono under it, and an unknown item reads "judge unsure". The trial header opens with a plain headline ("Failed the check nothing_for_others (over action)", "Passed every gate check."; an excluded trial keeps its "Not counted" line in that place). Under the verdict and the reason come "Why it failed" (the decisive check, what it requires and what the trial left), "Whose fault" (the fault as a chip linking to its definition, the kind of failure as a pill, one plain sentence and the rule that decided it) and "Also failed" (the secondary reason, muted). M1 to M5 collapse to "n of 5 confirmed", which opens to the codes, and the summary names any code not confirmed. The Attribution tab opens with its verdict ("Fault: environment, decided by E8", the criterion and its evidence). Confirmation reads "confirmed (M1–M5)", and every criterion checked is under "Show all criteria checked (N)". In the trajectory, thinking is plain muted text behind a rule with a small "Thinking" caption, tool names sit in a pill, and the scroll pane fades at its foot. Live trial: the running line has a spinner. The status line says "Starting" until the first model turn and gives the final state in the chips' words ("Finished: FAIL, fault: model", "Finished: API problem, not counted"), never a raw code. At phone width it does not repeat the Turn meter's number on screen; screen readers still hear it. Each trial row button is named with its run heading ("<run heading>, trial 1"). No task, grader or env version moves.
