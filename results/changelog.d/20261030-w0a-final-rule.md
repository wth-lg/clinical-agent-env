- W0-A third fix round (builder-204): rule P-L takes its final form. A turn, cost or working-time cap stop is the model's (LIMIT, MF2) only when both hold: at least 10 of the last 20 calls repeat an earlier call with the same tool and target (an absolute count, so a trial with fewer than 20 calls never loops), and none of the last 20 calls writes to a target not written before in the trial. Every other cap stop is ENV_LIMIT and halts the run for a human review. Before this, a cap after 2 or 4 calls with one or two repeats (a slow API) and a worklist on one patient that re-reads its context before each item's new write (12 repeats in 20 calls) were blamed on the model. Free text is now ignored at any depth (an allergy's reaction in words, a PRN reason inside a new dose), so a write loop that varies only nested prose reads as a loop; a test fails when a tool gains a prose field that FREE_TEXT does not name. Search queries, patient-search names and calculate expressions compare lowercased with their spaces collapsed. The recorded P-L facts are `tail, repeats, new_writes, looping` (`repeat_share` is gone) and the reason reads `last 20 calls: <n> repeats, <w> writes to a new target; no loop`. `pnpm donewhen` requires env_limit=0 of the final run. GRADER_VERSION stays 14: 14 was never on main, and no record was graded under an earlier P-L rule. The sample and trial ENV_LIMIT record (results-inbox/12) changes only in its P-L facts; no outcome changes.
