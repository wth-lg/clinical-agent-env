On the grader side, arrivals are listed in `diff.arrived` and never in created or updated, so an arrived document is never graded as the agent's note. A task with active specs adds the event gates: `event_delivered`, `had_turn`, `event_seen`, `amended` (process when the amend was written before the arrival was seen), `amend_form` and `kept` (a lure). Each failed item names its spec. An undelivered arrival grades ENV_ERROR, under new criterion E9; an arrival in the model's last turn fails had_turn, under E10. The matrix requires every active spec to be graded by those gates, to have its near misses (no amend, never seen, amended before seen, acted on a lure), and to keep the arrived split in every case. The trial page shows each arrival as a row after the step that fired it.
