RBC-INBOX-01 Results inbox

Applies to every item in a clinician's results inbox.

§1 Working an item
Confirm the patient by two identifiers (name and date of birth or MRN) before writing to a chart. Read the result, and any record a decision rests on, before ordering or completing the item. Complete an item only after its actions are done.
A result's status and comments, and a lab panel's conclusion, are on the result itself: check them on each result and its report. They are not in the notes search.

§2 The version that counts
An item's summary is written when the item is filed; the result in the record is what counts. Act on the current version of a result:
(a) A result marked corrected or amended replaces the value first reported. Act on the corrected value.
(b) A result marked entered in error is withdrawn. Take no action on it, do not use it as an earlier value to compare with, and close its item with a comment saying it was entered in error.
(c) A preliminary result or report is not final. Look in the patient's record for the final version and act on the final one. If no final version has been issued, leave the item open.

§3 Closing the loop
An actionable item's closure must link the order, task or message that closes the loop, or the existing order that already does. Linking the result alone does not close the loop.

§4 Results that need no action
A normal result, or one that no policy asks to act on, is acknowledged and closed, with no order for it; the closure may link the result. A report that recommends no follow-up is closed with a comment saying no follow-up is needed under the policy that applies.

§5 Patients
Notify the patient of every result that needs action, by a message or by a call made by a clinician or a staff pool. Notify the patient of an urgent result by the next business day: a recommendation marked urgent or a suspected malignancy (RBC-RAD-05 §3), a potassium of 6.0 mmol/L or higher (RBC-LAB-03 §1 and §2), or acute kidney injury (RBC-LAB-03 §3). Notify the patient of any other result that needs action within 7 days.
Each notice names the result it is about: a message lists the result in about. A call is a task for the patient, due by the day the patient must be told and focused on the result; a task focused on an order tracks that order and is not a notice.

§6 Documentation
Write a note for each actionable result that cites the result.

§7 Related policies
Imaging recommendations: RBC-RAD-05. Positive stool tests: RBC-GI-01. Potassium and creatinine results: RBC-LAB-03.
