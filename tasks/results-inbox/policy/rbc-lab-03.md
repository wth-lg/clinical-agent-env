RBC-LAB-03 Potassium and creatinine results

Applies to serum or plasma potassium (K) and creatinine results.

§1 K 6.0 mmol/L or higher, not hemolyzed
Notify pool-oncall at stat priority and order a stat BMP today. Review the patient's medications for any drug that raises potassium, and hold or reduce it where that is safe.

§2 K 6.0 mmol/L or higher, flagged hemolyzed
Hemolysis can raise a potassium result falsely. Order a repeat BMP (urgent) by the next business day after today, message the patient to come in, and do not notify on-call.

§3 Acute kidney injury (AKI)
A creatinine result meets the AKI rule when it is at least 0.3 mg/dL higher than a creatinine of the same patient collected in the 48 hours before it, or at least 1.5 times the lowest creatinine of the patient collected in the 7 days before it. Compare collection times, and use every valid creatinine in the window.
For a result that meets the AKI rule, order a repeat BMP (urgent) by the next business day after today and notify the patient. A creatinine that does not meet the rule needs no action under this section.
