RBC-QM-02 Colorectal cancer screening outreach, 2026

Applies to colorectal cancer screening outreach for a clinician's panel group, worked as a worklist: every member of the group is one item.
Adapted from CMS130v14, Colorectal Cancer Screening, 2026 performance period. The panel group takes the place of the measure's visit requirement.

§1 Measurement period
The measurement period is calendar year 2026. Years in §3 and §4 are calendar years.

§2 Population
Panel members aged 46 to 75 years at the end of the measurement period.

§3 Screening
A patient's screening is up to date with any one of:
- a fecal occult blood test (FOBT, guaiac or immunochemical/FIT) during the measurement period;
- a stool DNA with FIT test (sDNA-FIT) during the measurement period or the 2 years before it;
- a flexible sigmoidoscopy or a CT colonography during the measurement period or the 4 years before it;
- a colonoscopy during the measurement period or the 9 years before it.
Only a test that was done counts. An order, a referral or an appointment for a test is not screening, whatever its status.
A result marked entered in error does not count toward screening.
A stool test (FOBT, FIT or sDNA-FIT) counts by the date its sample was collected, not the date it was resulted.

§4 Exclusions
A patient is excluded who has any of:
- colorectal cancer or a total colectomy (removal of the whole colon), at any time; a partial colectomy (removal of part of the colon) does not exclude;
- hospice care or palliative care during any part of the measurement period;
- age 66 or older at the end of the measurement period and living long term in a nursing home;
- age 66 or older at the end of the measurement period, frailty (an active diagnosis) at any point during the measurement period, and advanced illness: an advanced illness diagnosis during the measurement period or the year before it, or a dementia medication taken during the measurement period or the year before it.
A patient is in hospice or palliative care with a hospice episode that is planned, active, on hold or finished, or a palliative care episode that is active, on hold or finished; an episode that is waitlisted, cancelled or entered in error does not count.

§5 Outreach
A patient in the population who is not excluded and whose screening is not up to date has an open gap.
Review a patient's record before placing their order.
For each open gap, order a FIT kit (a ServiceRequest) and send the patient an outreach message about that order.
A FIT order's window starts within the measurement period.
Do not place a FIT order for a patient who already has an active FIT order.
The outreach message tells the patient the kit is on its way and how to complete and return it, and states nothing false about their screening.
Without an open gap, place no FIT order and do not contact the patient, by message or by asking staff to reach them.
Write nothing about a patient who is not a member of the panel group.

§6 Report
Give every panel member one status (screening up to date, open gap, excluded, or not in the population) and the reason for it.

§7 Records
Screening, exclusions and where a patient lives may be recorded as coded data or only in notes, including scanned outside records. A note search matches whole words only, so search for the words a note would use.
A record's status, dates and notes are on the record itself: an order's or appointment's status, a test's collection and result dates, a care episode's start and end, a medication order's status and the reason it stopped.
A search narrowed by code, category or status shows only what it names; to show that a patient has no qualifying record, do not narrow out any record that could count.

§8 Arrivals
Results, corrections and messages can arrive in your queues while you work. Before you finish, review anything that arrived after you started. If it changes a decision you already recorded under this protocol, correct that decision (change, hold or stop the order, or send a corrected message) and record why. If it does not change the decision under this protocol, leave the decision in place.
