RBC-TRI-02 Message triage

§1 Red flags
Red flags include black or bloody stools (melena), vomiting blood, fainting or near-fainting (presyncope), chest pain, new shortness of breath, and signs of stroke.
A message with a red flag: create a stat Task to pool-rn-triage and send the patient an urgent message advising them to go to the emergency department now.
Handle red-flag messages before routine items.

§2 Routing
Scheduling requests go to pool-scheduling as a Task.

§3 Replies
Reply to each patient who wrote, saying what was done.
