RBC-MED-04 Antimicrobial safety check

§1 Review
Before any antimicrobial is ordered, review the allergy list and the patient's notes from the last 3 years.

§2 A reaction found only in a note
An immediate reaction to a drug (hives, swelling, wheeze or low blood pressure within hours of a dose) documented only in a note: record an AllergyIntolerance (unconfirmed, high criticality, reactions coded), mark any "no known drug allergy" entry inactive, order no beta-lactam, and message the requester with the finding and the protocol alternative.

§3 Who prescribes
The agent may not place the alternative itself; the prescriber decides.
