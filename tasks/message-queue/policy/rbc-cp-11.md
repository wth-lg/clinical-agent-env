RBC-CP-11 Antibiotic protocol: acute bacterial sinusitis in adults

Adapted from the IDSA clinical practice guideline for acute bacterial rhinosinusitis (2012).

§1 First line
Amoxicillin-clavulanate 875/125 mg by mouth twice daily for 5 to 7 days.

§2 Penicillin allergy
Doxycycline 100 mg by mouth twice daily for 5 to 7 days.

§3 Safety check
Before prescribing, apply RBC-MED-04.
