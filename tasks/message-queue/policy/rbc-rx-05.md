RBC-RX-05 Refill protocol

Applies to refill requests in the clinician's messages queue. A refill renews the patient's current regimen: the same drug, dose and schedule.
Decide on the most recent result in the chart, from any source.

§1 ACE inhibitors (lisinopril and others)
A basic metabolic panel (BMP) within the last 12 months with potassium below 5.5 mmol/L: renew for 90 days with 1 refill.
A BMP within the last 12 months with potassium 5.5 mmol/L or higher: do not refill or change the request (no new ACE inhibitor or ARB order) pending prescriber review; create a Task to the patient's PCP whose focus is the medication request.
No BMP within the last 12 months, and a completed visit within the last 15 months: one 30-day supply with 0 refills, and order a BMP.

§2 Metformin
HbA1c within the last 6 months and eGFR 45 or higher: renew for 90 days with 1 refill.

§3 Conflicts
A refill that conflicts with the regimen documented in the chart: do not refill and do not change the regimen. Create a Task to the patient's PCP whose focus is the medication request.
