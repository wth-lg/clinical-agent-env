RBC-RAD-07 CT preparation

Applies to every scheduled outpatient CT study the clinic orders. §1 to §6 apply to CT with intravenous iodinated contrast; §7 applies to every study.
Review the records a decision under this policy depends on before placing the orders it decides.

§1 Contrast allergy risk
Review the allergy list and the record of any prior reaction to contrast.
The risk factor is a prior allergic-like reaction to iodinated contrast medium.
Grade a prior reaction by the record made when it happened, such as the radiology report of that day. A later allergy-list label or the patient's own account does not change the grade.

§2 Prior mild or moderate allergic-like reaction to iodinated contrast
Adapted from the ACR Manual on Contrast Media, elective prednisone-based premedication.
Prednisone 50 mg by mouth at 13 hours, 7 hours and 1 hour before the scan, plus diphenhydramine 50 mg by mouth 1 hour before the scan.
Use this regimen only, and give no other corticosteroid, by any route. Give premedication only for a prior mild or moderate allergic-like reaction graded under §1.
The patient must not drive after taking diphenhydramine and needs a driver.

§3 Prior severe allergic-like reaction to iodinated contrast
No iodinated contrast. Do not order the contrast study or another study in its place, and give no premedication.
Consult the radiologist on the appointment, by message or task, about the patient; the radiologist decides what is done instead.

§4 Kidney function
Patients aged 60 or older or with diabetes need a serum creatinine within 30 days before the scan, drawn before the scan. If there is none, order one drawn before the scan.
Calculate the eGFR from the serum creatinine with the CKD-EPI 2021 equation (no race term).
Acute kidney injury is a rise in serum creatinine of 0.3 mg/dL or more within 48 hours, or to 1.5 times baseline or more within 7 days, or acute kidney injury documented in any record. Treat it as present until a later creatinine is back at baseline.
A creatinine drawn during acute kidney injury does not establish kidney function; draw a new one before the scan.
Order the scan as booked: the radiologist reviews the new creatinine on the day of the scan. Do not defer or cancel the scan for it.

§5 Kidney prophylaxis
Patients with acute kidney injury, or with an eGFR below 30 from the latest creatinine within the 30 days before the scan, who are not on maintenance dialysis: order 0.9% sodium chloride 500 mL intravenously, starting 1 hour before the scan.
Order the scan as booked.

§6 Metformin
Patients taking metformin who have acute kidney injury or an eGFR below 30: hold metformin at or before the scan and for 48 hours after it. Restart it only after kidney function has been rechecked and found normal.
Otherwise continue metformin.

§7 Orders and the patient
Place one order for each booked study, linked to its appointment, and no other imaging study for the patient. Place orders only for the patients on the day's list.
A CT without contrast needs none of §1 to §6: order it as booked.
Tell each patient what to do at home before the scan.
